Privacy

Surfboard Designer · last updated November 15, 2026

The short version

Surfboard Designer does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Your designs

The boards you design are saved only on your own device. They leave it only when you choose to export or share a file yourself.

Feedback

The Feedback button prepares a message containing what you typed and, if you leave the box ticked, the measurements of the board you have open. Nothing is sent anywhere until you send that message yourself.

Fonts

When you are online, the app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Google receives the request for the font files, as it would for any website that uses them. The app works without them when you are offline.
